Pre-Listing Checklist

Tap each item as you finish it. Progress saves in your browser this session. The good news: a 2023 home doesn't need much — focus on the staging and lifestyle moments that show off The Miller's open layout and your North Pointe lifestyle.

Curb Appeal & Entry 0 of 5
  • Pressure-wash front porch, walkway, and visible siding
  • Edge beds, fresh mulch, seasonal color in planters by the front door
  • New welcome mat, polished front door hardware, fresh paint if dinged
  • Sweep sidewalk in front of the home — North Pointe's walkability is part of the sell
  • Wipe down house numbers, light fixtures, and porch ceiling fan blades
Foyer & Entry 0 of 3
  • Clear the foyer — no shoes, bags, mail, or clutter on entry surface
  • One simple console with a lamp, mirror, or art — buyers form first impression here
  • Wipe down the transom window and any glass — make the natural light pop
Kitchen 0 of 6
  • Clear all granite counters — keep only 1 or 2 intentional items
  • Deep-clean appliances inside and out (oven, fridge, microwave, dishwasher)
  • Empty maple cabinets to 70% — buyers open them
  • Style the island — a fresh fruit bowl or cookbook on a stand sells the vignette
  • Organize the pantry — a tidy walk-in pantry is a major selling point
  • Fresh dish towels, trash can hidden, eat-in nook cleared and staged
Dining Room 0 of 3
  • Set the table simply — neutral runner or placemats, no full place settings
  • Fresh flowers or greenery centerpiece
  • Dust chandelier, replace any out bulbs — modern fixtures should sparkle
Great Room (First Floor) 0 of 4
  • Remove 30–50% of furniture if the room feels crowded — open layout is the story
  • Anchor with a rug, fluff pillows, fresh throws in 2 coordinated colors
  • Bookshelves at 70% — stack horizontally with white space
  • Wipe TV screens, hide cords, draw attention up toward the tray/vaulted ceiling
Mud Room & Half Bath 0 of 3
  • Clear hooks of jackets and bags — keep only 1 or 2 styled items
  • Shoe storage hidden or tidied — bench or floor area cleared
  • Half bath: clear counter, fresh hand towel, single candle or soap pump
Stairwell & Upstairs Landing 0 of 3
  • Polish railings, vacuum stair runners or treads
  • Clear the landing — no laundry, no boxes, no toys
  • Wipe down the upstairs laundry room — it's a key selling point on the bedroom level
Primary Suite & Walk-In Closet 0 of 6
  • White or neutral linens, hotel-style bed
  • Walk-in closet at 70% capacity — buyers open every door
  • Clear nightstands except one lamp and one book
  • Fresh flowers or small plant on the dresser
  • Remove personal photos and replace with neutral art
  • Primary bath: dual vanity cleared, fresh white towels, polished fixtures
Secondary Bedrooms & Hall Bath 0 of 4
  • Make every bed daily with neutral linens
  • Clear all surfaces, hide laundry hampers
  • Closets at 70%, shoes lined up neatly
  • Hall bath: clear counters, fresh towels, polish fixtures
Unfinished Basement (448 sqft) 0 of 4
  • Sweep / clear the space — present it as expansion potential, not storage
  • Bright lighting on, no boxes blocking sight lines
  • Tape a simple sign: "Future Rec Room / Office / 4th Bedroom potential"
  • Run a dehumidifier the morning of showings — fresh-smelling space sells the upside
Garage (2-Car Attached) 0 of 4
  • Sweep floor, organize tools and bikes on walls if possible
  • Trash cans tucked to one side, not center stage
  • Test the auto-door opener — make sure it's smooth and quiet
  • Wipe down any oil spots or stains on the concrete floor
Backyard & Patio 0 of 4
  • Mow, edge, and weed-eat the week of listing
  • Power-wash the patio
  • Stage outdoor seating — fresh cushions, planter, throw pillow
  • Add a string of bistro lights if you have them — it photographs beautifully at twilight
Day-of Showing 0 of 6
  • Open every blind and curtain, turn on every light — show off the Low-E windows
  • Soft music playing, neutral scent (skip plug-ins — fresh-brewed coffee is better)
  • Thermostat at 70°F
  • Trash cans out of sight, pet items + bowls put away
  • Lock up any prescriptions, jewelry, and valuables
  • Leave the home — buyers can't relax with the seller present

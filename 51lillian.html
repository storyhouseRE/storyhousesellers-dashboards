Pre-Listing Checklist

Tap each item as you finish it. Progress saves in your browser this session. No pressure to do every item — focus on the ones Brentney circles during the walkthrough.

Curb Appeal & Entry 0 of 5
  • Pressure-wash front walk, porch, and cedar siding
  • Edge beds, fresh mulch, add seasonal color (pansies or annuals near entry)
  • New welcome mat, fresh paint on front door if dinged
  • Polish or replace front door hardware, repaint mailbox post
  • Clear gutters, touch-up cedar siding stain where weathered
Kitchen 0 of 5
  • Clear all countertops — keep only 1 or 2 intentional items
  • Deep-clean appliances inside and out (oven, fridge, microwave, dishwasher)
  • Empty cabinets to 70% — buyers open them
  • Polish cabinet hardware, replace dated knobs if needed
  • Fresh dish towels out, trash can hidden, eat-in area cleared and staged
Primary Suite & Walk-In Closet 0 of 5
  • White or neutral linens, hotel-style bed
  • Walk-in closet at 70% capacity — buyers open every door
  • Clear nightstands except one lamp and one book
  • Fresh flowers or small plant on the dresser
  • Remove personal photos and replace with neutral art
Bathrooms 0 of 5
  • Clear counters — only soap dispenser and one candle
  • Fresh white towels, rolled or folded crisply
  • Re-caulk shower/tub seams if any look tired
  • Polish all fixtures, deep-clean glass and ceramic tile
  • Hide toothbrushes, products, scales — bath should look like a hotel
Family Room 0 of 4
  • Remove 30–50% of furniture if the room feels crowded
  • Anchor with a rug, fluff pillows, fresh throws
  • Bookshelves at 70% — stack horizontally with white space
  • Wipe TV screens, hide cords
Additional Bedrooms 0 of 4
  • Make every bed daily with neutral linens
  • Clear all surfaces, hide laundry hampers
  • Closets at 70%, shoes lined up neatly
  • Remove personal photos or replace frames with neutral art
Crawlspace & Utility Areas 0 of 3
  • Make sure the conditioned crawlspace is dry and accessible — buyers WILL ask to see it
  • Have receipts for crawlspace conditioning work + water heater install ready
  • Replace HVAC filter the week of listing
Backyard, Deck & Storage Shed 0 of 6
  • Mow, edge, and weed-eat the week of listing — fenced lot looks crisp
  • Power-wash the expanded back deck and re-stain if needed
  • Stage outdoor seating on the deck — fresh cushions, planter, throw
  • Organize the storage shed — buyers love that it has electricity, but it has to look usable
  • Walk the fence line — repair any sections, oil the gate hinges
  • Check garden space — even one well-kept raised bed adds story
Day-of Showing 0 of 6
  • Open every blind and curtain, turn on every light
  • Soft music playing, neutral scent (skip plug-ins — fresh-brewed coffee is better)
  • Thermostat at 70°F
  • Trash cans out of sight, pet items + bowls put away
  • Lock up any prescriptions, jewelry, and valuables
  • Leave the home — buyers can't relax with the seller present

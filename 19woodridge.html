Story House Staging Playbook

The canonical pre-listing prep guide — work through each area at your own pace.

Curb Appeal & Entry
  • Pressure-wash front walk, porch, and exterior siding
  • Edge beds, mulch, replace tired plantings with seasonal color
  • New welcome mat, fresh paint on front door if dinged
  • Polish or replace front door hardware, repaint mailbox post
  • Clear gutters, touch-up trim paint where peeling
Kitchen
  • Clear all countertops — keep only one or two intentional items
  • Deep-clean appliances inside and out (oven, fridge, microwave)
  • Empty cabinets to 70% — buyers open them
  • Polish hardware, replace dated knobs if needed
  • Fresh dish towels, hide trash can
Primary Suite
  • White or neutral linens, hotel-style bed
  • Closet at 70% — buyers will open every door
  • Clear nightstands except one lamp and one book
  • Fresh flowers or small plant on the dresser
Primary Bath
  • Clear counters — only soap dispenser, one candle
  • Fresh white towels rolled or folded crisply
  • Re-caulk if any seams look tired
  • Polish all fixtures, deep-clean glass
Living & Family Rooms
  • Remove 30–50% of furniture if rooms feel crowded
  • Anchor with a rug, fluff pillows, fresh throws
  • Bookshelves at 70% — stack horizontally with breaks
  • Wipe TV screens, hide cords
Dining Room
  • Set the table simply — neutral runner, no full place settings
  • Fresh flowers or greenery centerpiece
  • Dust chandelier, replace any out bulbs
Bedrooms & Closets
  • Make every bed daily with neutral linens
  • Clear all surfaces, hide laundry
  • Closets at 70%, shoes lined up
  • Remove personal photos from frames or replace with art
Basement / Rec Room
  • Bright lighting, no overflow storage on display
  • Fresh-smelling dehumidifier running before showings
  • Clean carpet or area rug if applicable
Outdoor Spaces & Acreage
  • Mow, edge, weed-eat the week of listing
  • Power-wash deck and patio
  • Stage outdoor seating area — fresh cushions, candles, throw
  • Clear pool/water features of debris, run before showings
  • Walk the property line — clear fallen limbs, mow accessible trails
Day-of Showing
  • Open every blind and curtain, turn on every light
  • Soft music, neutral scent (avoid plug-ins — fresh-baked bread or coffee is better)
  • Temperature at 70°F
  • Trash cans in the garage, pet items out of sight
  • Leave the home — buyers can't relax with sellers present

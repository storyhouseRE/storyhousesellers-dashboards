Neighborhood Snapshot

Chesterfield Landing is a Stanley Martin community in Crozet built primarily 2015-2020. Walkable to Downtown Crozet (Crozet Library, Mudhouse Coffee, Crozet Pizza, Pro Re Nata Brewery), close to Claudius Crozet Park, and minutes to Old Trail Village amenities. Recent comparable sales on Watervale Drive: 5510 ($685K, 4BR/3.5BA, 3,292sqft, 2022), 5534 ($839K, 5BR/4.5BA, 3,292sqft, 2023), 5552 ($940K, 4BR/2.5BA, 3,112sqft, 2024). The street currently shows strong appreciation. School zone: Crozet Elementary, Henley Middle, Western Albemarle High — consistently top-rated in Albemarle County.
